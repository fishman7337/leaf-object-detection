# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This repository supports academic, engineering, and reproducibility work. All
participants are expected to communicate respectfully, give constructive
technical feedback, and acknowledge uncertainty clearly.

Examples of welcome behavior:

- Treat questions and mistakes as part of the research process.
- Keep critiques focused on evidence, methods, code, and documentation.
- Credit prior work, collaborators, institutions, and reused materials.
- Protect student, lecturer, reviewer, and contributor privacy.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private contact details, credentials, datasets, or grading
  information without permission.
- Misrepresenting results, authorship, institutional endorsement, or licensing.
- Pressuring contributors to bypass research ethics, safety, or reproducibility
  expectations.

## Scope

This code applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, notebooks,
documentation, datasets, model artifacts, and any related project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove harmful content, close discussions, reject
contributions, or restrict participation. Concerns should be reported privately
to the repository maintainer where possible, with enough context to reproduce
or understand the issue.
